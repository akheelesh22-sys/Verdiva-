Rooted in Mauritius

A little closer to the people who grow it.

Verdiva Ltd is a local basket delivery business based at Temple Road, Rivière du Rempart. We bring good food from the hands that grow and prepare it to the homes that love it.

We work with local Mauritian planters and certified poultry and meat suppliers. Each basket is harvested and packed fresh on delivery day, so it can travel directly to you without waiting in a cold-storage warehouse.
